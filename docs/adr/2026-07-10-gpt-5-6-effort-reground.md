# ADR 2026-07-10 — GPT-5.6 effort ladder v2: historical exception tiers, current routing policy

**Status:** accepted as a record of the 2026-07-10 ladder; live effort routing is governed by `docs/codex-routing.md` §3.
**Context (historical).** GPT-5.6 added `max` reasoning effort and `ultra` multi-subagent mode above xhigh, falsifying three doctrine surfaces that said "Codex tops out at xhigh," while recipes hardcoded blanket xhigh (known 009 D8 follow-up). The 5.6 system card reports increased agentic-coding overreach concentrated at highest effort under persistence-heavy prompts; METR reports its highest detected ReAct-harness cheating rate (prompt/scaffold-dependent). Installed surfaces at acceptance: companion v1.0.5 rejects effort >xhigh; codex CLI 0.144.0; catalog advertised max/ultra (ultra listed for Terra too — tier availability was a preflight fact, not press-derived).

**Decision at acceptance (historical).** (1) Dispatch-time ladder: medium/high/xhigh operating band on `gpt-5.6-sol`; every Codex unit declared effort explicitly — omitted effort was a validation error, never a default. (2) `max`/`ultra` were exception tiers: opt-in per unit, non-empty justification echoed into the dispatch receipt, never a default anywhere. (3) `ultra` was hard-rejected in `.wf.js` recipes until three controls existed: actual-token telemetry, ×4 concurrency accounting, one instrumented run. (4) A tier a surface rejected surfaced a failure; the then-specified automatic “degrade unit to Claude” action is retired with the worker/recipe path and is not a current fallback. (5) The six-field work-order contract — goal, paths, constraints, non-goals, proof command, and output shape — plus verbatim honest reporting belonged in every runnable prompt builder. (6) Review of executor output was never delegated to the executor that produced the diff and was never skipped. (7) Phase B was evidence-gated: run when at least two independent non-OpenAI evaluations were directionally concordant, or on the 2026-08-15 expiry with the shortfall recorded; then re-run the consensus sweep and re-answer the topology question. Until that trigger or expiry, the frozen 2026-07-10 §2 snapshot remained byte-untouched behind the dated stale banner: SWE-bench Pro 64.3% versus 58.6%; blind review 67% versus 25%; approximately 3–4× cheaper and $155 versus $15 cost proxies; SWE-bench Verified 87.6% versus 88.7%.

## Current policy

`docs/codex-routing.md` §3 controls live direct-Codex effort selection: every command names its effort explicitly; medium, high, and xhigh are the operating band; `max` is retired and unselectable; and attended direct-CLI `ultra` remains separately admitted where the installed CLI exposes it. A selected provider failure stays visible and fails closed. A Claude or other-lane dispatch requires an explicit caller/operator selection; no rejected tier or unavailable provider authorizes an automatic rerun.

**Consequences.** This ADR preserves the evidence for explicit effort, receipt justification, and the former recipe constraints. The old exception-tier admission, recipe contract, and automatic Claude-degrade rule are historical only. Current dispatch fails on omitted effort and follows the live band and attended-`ultra` rule above; the strength table still cannot absorb launch-day claims.
