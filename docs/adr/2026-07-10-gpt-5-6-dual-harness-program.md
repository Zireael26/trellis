# ADR 2026-07-10 — GPT-5.6 adoption program: effort re-ground, follow-ups convention, Codex parallel orchestration

**Status:** accepted for Specs 011/012 and deliberate direct `codex exec` routing; partially superseded by the Spec 013 retirement in commit `2ad1808`. The race-the-legs decision was separately retired by `2026-07-10-sol-ultra-capability-reground.md`. Its 2026-07-10 `xhigh`+`max` band is historical only; live direct-Codex effort policy is `docs/codex-routing.md` §3: explicit medium/high/xhigh, `max` retired, and attended direct-CLI `ultra` only.
**Context (historical).** OpenAI shipped GPT-5.6 (Sol/Terra/Luna) publicly 2026-07-09: new `max` reasoning effort and `ultra` multi-subagent mode, ~half Fable-5 token price on Sol, and a system card documenting increased agentic-coding overreach (most pronounced at highest effort under persistence-heavy prompts). Three Trellis doctrine surfaces carried then-false "no max for Codex" ceiling claims; recipes hardcoded blanket xhigh; the fire-and-forget Codex dispatch path broke workflow barriers in production (the polyglot monorepo spec 037). The operator's directive at acceptance was gpt-5.6-sol as the Codex model, medium→xhigh operating band, and `max`/`ultra` as exception tiers. This dated record does not set the current routing policy.

**Decision (2026-07-10 program).** Three-spec program, one intake. The effort-tier and recipe portions below record the accepted program; live tier selection defers to `docs/codex-routing.md` §3.
- **Spec 011 (historical effort-tier detail)** — effort ladder v2 (explicit-effort-or-error, then-reachable exception tiers receipt-justified, ultra locked out of recipes until telemetry + ×4 cap + instrumented run), stale ceiling claims killed on active surfaces (historical artifacts exempt), safety clauses landed in then-runnable prompt builders (not just prose), §2 strength table re-ground evidence-gated (≥2 independent non-OpenAI evals, expiry 2026-08-15). Codex adversarial review (Sol xhigh) verdict RETHINK folded as r2. The historical `max`/`ultra` and recipe treatment does not admit a live `max` path.
- **Spec 012** — follow-ups convention: automatic prioritized follow-ups at spec/PR/DoD boundaries, zero-extra-token derivation, spec-section + root-ledger capture, non-blocking stop-verify warn, route-by-size disposition.
- **Spec 013 (worker/fanout portion historical; retired by commit `2ad1808`)** — rc.10 added a blocking `codex-worker` canonical agent and `codex-fanout` recipe (the the polyglot monorepo handoff: 30s poll / 15-min stall / one retry / FAILURE report; unattended preamble after the collaboration-tool stall root-cause), plus six dual-harness speed patterns (race-the-legs, cross-harness pipelined verify, warm-thread pool, primer-fed dispatch, streaming merges, ultra-as-node). The retired worker, fanout, preflight, and rollout surfaces are historical evidence only, not current routing or acceptance guidance.
The companion doctrine flip remains: active supported workflows prefer dynamic workflows over named teammates for fan-out because engine agents auto-terminate while named teammates require explicit `TaskStop`. The 2026-07-10 node/PATH hook outage was fixed for the Codex-plugin invokers; current per-plugin re-break caveats are recorded in `gotchas.md` under “Teammate node/PATH hook outage fixed at the invoker.”

**Consequences.** Specs 011/012 preserve the explicit-effort and follow-up records, but current direct Codex dispatch follows `docs/codex-routing.md` §3: medium/high/xhigh are the operating band, `max` is retired, and attended direct-CLI `ultra` remains separately available. Safety and strength-routing claims stay evidence-gated; every completion boundary emits durable, prioritized follow-ups. A selected provider failure remains visible and fails closed; another provider or lane runs only after explicit caller/operator selection. Direct Codex work remains deliberate `codex exec` routing, with no Trellis-owned worker or recipe mediating it.

> **Historical Spec 013 consequences.** In rc.10, Codex units became first-class workflow nodes with pattern parity to Claude agents, and the polling worker made stalls bounded failures with receipts rather than silent hangs. That implementation carried a custom agent, fanout recipe, preflight, rollout, and per-checkout warm-thread state to maintain. Commit `2ad1808` retired those surfaces; preserve this consequence as field evidence, not as current architecture.
